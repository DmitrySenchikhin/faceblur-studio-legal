Support

Effective October 9, 2026

Contact

For help with FaceBlur Studio on iPhone or iPad, open a support request. Include the device, iOS version, and what happened. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or photos and videos of people. A GitHub request is public.

Pro is not active

If Pro does not unlock after a purchase, open the Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that paid.

Cancel renewal

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then FaceBlur Studio, then Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscriptions. Apple handles billing questions for the subscription. Lifetime Pro does not renew.

Delete a project

Deleting a project removes it and the app’s copy of its media from this device. Deleting the app removes the app’s local storage. Neither action cancels a subscription.
