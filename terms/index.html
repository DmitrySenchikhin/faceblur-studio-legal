The app

FaceBlur Studio is a tool for blurring or covering people and other sensitive details in photos and videos you select. You need the rights to the media you import. Review every export before you share it. Automatic detection can miss a face, object, or word.

Free and Pro

Without Pro, photo saving is limited, saved photos can include a “Made with FaceBlur Studio” mark, and video export stays locked. Pro removes those limits and unlocks the paid tools shown in the app, including all effects, sticker packs, Batch Photos, and full video export.

What you buy

Monthly Pro and Yearly Pro are auto-renewable subscriptions. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase and does not renew. There is no free trial. Payment starts when you confirm the purchase.

Length and price

Monthly Pro lasts 1 month and costs 3.99 USD. Yearly Pro lasts 1 year and costs 19.99 USD. Lifetime Pro costs 49.99 USD once. Apple charges the price shown on the purchase screen when you confirm. The price can differ by country and currency.

Auto-renewal

A monthly or yearly plan renews automatically for the same length. Apple charges your Apple ID when you confirm, and again within 24 hours before the current period ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. Lifetime Pro does not renew.

How to cancel

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then FaceBlur Studio, then Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscriptions. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Restore

Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings reloads a plan already purchased with this Apple ID.

Price changes

If the price changes, Apple asks you to agree before charging the new price.
