Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Media stays on the device

FaceBlur Studio edits photos and videos you choose. Those files are copied into the app’s private storage on this iPhone or iPad so a project can be reopened. FaceBlur Studio does not upload your photos, videos, or recordings to a server operated for this app. There is no FaceBlur Studio account.

On-device analysis

Face and object detection, text recognition, background separation, blur, and the other privacy tools use Apple frameworks on the device. Speech tools request on-device speech recognition for captions and for finding words to censor. If an offline recognizer is not available, that speech step stops. The app does not send the audio to a transcription service in order to finish it.

Camera, microphone, and Photos

Live Camera uses the camera, and the microphone when you allow it, only while you are recording in the app. The recording stays in the app until you save it. Saving an export adds the file to your Photo Library. FaceBlur Studio does not read your whole library in the background.

Export details

When “Strip metadata” is on, an export is saved without location and camera details. Turning it off keeps those details in the exported file. A free photo save can include the text “Made with FaceBlur Studio”. That mark is drawn into the picture on the device.

Settings stored on the device

The app remembers choices such as language, appearance, whether onboarding is finished, and how many free photo saves have been used. Those values stay in the app’s local storage. They are not sent to a FaceBlur Studio server.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. FaceBlur Studio receives the purchase status needed to unlock Pro. It does not receive your card number.

Tracking and analytics

This app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics service operated for FaceBlur Studio. It does not sell personal information.

Deletion

Deleting a project removes that project and the app’s copy of its source media from this device. Deleting the app removes its local storage, as iOS does for any app. Neither action cancels an App Store subscription.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request. Do not attach photos or videos of people.
